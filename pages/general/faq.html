Frequently Asked Questions

Got questions? We've got answers. Learn more about booking and staying with CozyCorner.

How do I make a reservation?
Making a reservation is easy! Simply browse our Available Properties, select your desired check-in and check-out dates, and indicate the number of guests. Once you find a property you love, click "Book Now" and follow the secure checkout process.
What are the standard Check-in and Check-out times?
Standard check-in time begins at 2:00 PM local time, and check-out is strictly by 11:00 AM. If you need a late checkout or early check-in, you can purchase it as an add-on during the checkout process (subject to availability).
How do I get the keys to the property?
Most of our properties offer seamless Self Check-in. You will receive a secure digital code or smart lockbox combination via email 24 hours prior to your arrival. For select premium properties, a host will meet you at the lobby to hand over the physical keys.
What is your cancellation policy?
You can cancel your booking for a full refund up to 7 days before your check-in date. Cancellations made within 7 days of the check-in date are subject to a 50% cancellation fee. Same-day cancellations or no-shows are non-refundable.
Are pets allowed in the units?
Pet policies vary by property. Some of our Cozy Cabins and Mountain Views are fully pet-friendly, while City Lofts generally have strict no-pet rules due to building administration policies. Please leave a note in the "Special Requests" field during checkout if you plan to bring a pet.
What if I break something during my stay?
We understand accidents happen! Please report any damages to our Support Queue immediately. Minor breakages (like a shattered glass) are usually covered, but significant damage to furniture or appliances will be billed to the payment method on file.
Are parties or events allowed?
To maintain the quality of our properties and respect our neighbors, parties and large events are strictly prohibited. The maximum guest count specified on your booking must not be exceeded. Violations may result in immediate eviction without a refund.
