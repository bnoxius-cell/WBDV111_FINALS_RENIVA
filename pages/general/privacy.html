Privacy Policy

How we handle and protect your data.

1. Information We Collect

When you register for an account or book a stay, we collect personal details such as your full name, email address, phone number, and reservation dates. We may also collect standard browsing data to enhance your web experience.

2. How We Use Your Information

The information we collect is primarily used to process your bookings, communicate reservation details to your host, and provide 24/7 customer support. We also use aggregated, anonymized data to improve our platform's UI and booking features.

3. Data Security

We employ strict, industry-standard security measures to safeguard your personal data. All sensitive information, including user credentials and booking histories, is protected against unauthorized access, alteration, or destruction.
