Our Story

From a single spare room to a curated collection of premium stays, discover how GilMars Airbnb became your ultimate home away from home.

How It All Started

Back in 2020, Gilmar opened the doors of a humble spare room in Marilao, Bulacan, to a tired traveler. What started as a simple way to earn extra income quickly blossomed into a profound passion for hospitality. The realization was simple: travelers aren't just looking for a place to sleep; they are looking for an experience, a sanctuary, and a place to create lasting memories.

Today, GilMars Airbnb (lovingly known as CozyCorner) has grown into a premier network of beachfront resorts, city lofts, and mountain cabins. Every property is handpicked, rigorously verified, and uniquely designed to offer a flawless blend of contemporary minimalism and undeniable warmth.
